  2×200m Freestyle A1 — 50 ZRC / 50 swim

8×50m Backstroke A1 — Drill with fins (400m)

Pre-Set (1,000m)
10×100m Freestyle A1 — 75 streamline kick w/snorkel, 25 swim free

Main Set (1,000m)
2×200m IM @3:30 Z2 — Maintain 85% speed
4×100m Best Non-Free @2:15 Z2 — Maintain 85% speed
4×50m Freestyle @1:30 Z4 — Race pace 200

Total: 3,600m · 1h20m

Analysis

This is an aerobic maintenance day with a technical warm-up focus and a main set that layers stroke variety onto controlled effort. The 85% speed cap on the IM and non-free work keeps swimmers honest on technique before the 50s free let them touch race-pace speed for a 200.
